Earth

Earth is the third planet from the Sun and the only astronomical body known to harbor life.

  • Average Distance from the Sun: ~149.6 million kilometers (93 million miles)
  • Diameter: ~12,756 kilometers (7,926 miles)
  • Orbit (Length of Year): ~365.25 days
  • Rotation (Length of Day): 23.9 hours
